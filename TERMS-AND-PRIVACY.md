## Local-first data and optional connections

Vantage is local-first. Its preferences, derived timer and parser state, and
the game log data it reads are kept on your computer unless you deliberately
enable or use a feature that connects elsewhere. Vantage does not require a
Vantage account, and the Vantage project does not sell your data.

Depending on the features you choose, Vantage may make network connections for:

- application and VantageUI update checks or downloads from GitHub;
- community reference or guild data from sources such as the Project 1999
  Wiki, PigParse/EqTool data, OpenDKP, or a guild-provided spreadsheet;
- the LAN-only Mobile Host, which serves selected Vantage information to a
  device you pair on your local network; and
- optional Device Sync, which uses Syncthing as an encrypted device-to-device
  transport between PCs you explicitly pair.

Those providers and community resources have their own terms and privacy
practices. Vantage does not control them. If you enable Mobile Host or Device
Sync, you are responsible for protecting pairing links, secrets, paired
devices, and firewall or router settings. Share access only with
people and devices you trust.
