Built around the way you play

Less window hunting. More awareness.

One lightweight companion turns readable log data into the information you need while the game stays in focus.

SMART TIMERS

Spawns that understand the whole encounter.

Named deaths start zone-aware timers automatically. Add kill time, reset intelligently, color-code every bar, and keep manual control one click away.

SPELL AWARENESS

Buffs, debuffs, fades, and clickies.

Correct classic icons, skill-based colors, worn-off alerts, per-target tracking, and custom sounds with volume control.

COMBAT INSIGHT

Damage and healing without the clutter.

Review fights, pet contribution, criticals, resist activity, and heal chains while Vantage keeps the original log intact.

MARKET + LIBRARY

Research gear and spells in one place.

Filter items by class, race, slot, era, stats, effects, and drop status. Browse spells by class and level with acquisition details.

OVERLAYS + PHONE

Your layout, wherever you need it.

Pin compact windows over the game, place alerts precisely, or scan a QR code to follow timers and market data from your phone.

No account, injected game code, or heavy background service. Vantage reads the text logs you already control.
