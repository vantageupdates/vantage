THE COMPANION, BEYOND TIMERS

From the next spawn
to the next raid.

Open only the tools you need from the Quick Bar. Windows are movable, resizable and can use compact presets.

01

WHILE YOU PLAY

Keep the important moments in sight.

Buffs & Triggers
Track buffs and effects by character. Set fading stops and choose beeps, speech, custom WAVs or Off for each alert.
Smart Timers
Zone-aware spawn timers, placeholder lists, cloning and editable keyword commands. Share timers or keep separate timer windows.
Combat & Heal Chain
Review saved fights, damage and healing. Coordinate Complete Heal announcements with settings in the CH window.
Random Parser & Log Searcher
Read /random results with valid ranges. Search character logs for conversations, deaths and raid keywords.
Vitals Monitor
Calibrate a screen region over visible HP or mana numbers and set alert stops. Readings depend on font, capture and calibration.
02

EXPLORE NORRATH

Plan the camp. Follow the quest.

Interactive maps
Locate yourself from /loc, browse map labels and center a point of interest. Supported mob labels connect to loot research.
Zones & mob search
Choose a zone and browse mobs, named encounters, items and quests. Search for mobs beyond the selected zone.
Quests
Read quest information and track detailed steps in a floating checklist, including needed loot and encounters.
Skills & Spells
Browse by class and level, with skill acquisition, spells and linked effects. Source links stay available for reference.
03

MARKET & YOUR GEAR

Research more than the price.

Item details & comparisons
Game-inspired stat cards with weapon damage/delay, linked effects, drop and quest references. Compare items inside their detail window.
Market & sale alerts
Browse Green/Blue prices and track watched items. Search auctions heard by your client; live auction logging requires being in the EC Tunnel.
WTS / WTB tools
Build trade lists, choose an in-game social page/button, and copy a line-by-line Discord listing with item links.
Inventory & bank
Import EverQuest output dumps, find items across characters and edit the app’s tracked list. This does not remove items in the game.
04

GUILD DKP & MORE

See the raid history that belongs to you.

OpenDKP for your guild
Connect your guild’s OpenDKP address. Browse characters, balances, raids, loot and available auction information with search and sorting.
My raids with alts
Save a character group and search recorded attendance across a date range. Shared raids and tick IDs count once, with participating names shown.
Raid evidence
Check a public tick roster for your group, or review local log evidence. Missing award values stay unknown; balances are not combined or modified.
Guild spreadsheets
Add supported shared or published Google Sheets as named windows for other guild lists. Sheet access depends on the guild’s sharing settings.
05

MAKE IT YOURS

Your layout. Your alerts. Your second screen.

Quick Bar & Sounds
Attributable written notices, a master volume slider and a central Sounds window. Per-trigger mute preserves detection and visual notices.
Floating notes
Keep notes inside the app or turn them into compact sticky windows, with item links and quest references.
Linked PCs
Pair your computers without a separate Vantage account. Choose whether to sync buffs and timers; machine-specific paths remain local.
Mobile companion
Open the phone view from Vantage’s QR code for timers, buffs, market, guild tools, zones and quests. Save it to your home screen; live data needs a reachable running host.
VantageUI & character layouts
Install or update the in-game skin separately. Companion can audit character UI selections, copy a layout to chosen characters and restore saved backups.

START WITH YOUR LOGS

Ready for your next session.

  1. 01

    Run Companion

    Download Vantage.exe and open it on Windows. Updates are available from the Quick Bar.

  2. 02

    Enable EverQuest logging

    Type /log on in-game. Vantage uses the lines your client can see—not every event happening in the zone.

  3. 03

    Find your logs

    Use automatic EverQuest folder discovery or choose a folder manually. Then open your tools from the Quick Bar.

Want the in-game skin too?

VantageUI is optional. Use Quick Bar → Updates → VantageUI or its standalone updater, select your EverQuest folder and follow the load command shown after installation. Back up character settings before copying layouts.
